# 明膳知源（ClearDiet）Project Vision

## Product Positioning

**明膳知源**（ClearDiet；原 NutriSphere）is an educational knowledge graph for ordinary people.

It helps the general public conveniently look up and understand relationships between **diet and health**, and between **diet and disease**, in a clear, visual way.

### Who it is for
- Ordinary users (not nutrition professionals, clinicians, or researchers as the primary audience).
- People who want a straightforward way to explore how dietary patterns, foods, and biological mechanisms are discussed in relation to common health topics.

### What it is not
- Not a professional research platform for experts.
- Not a medical device, diagnostic tool, or source of personalized treatment advice.
- Not a place that conducts original nutrition or disease research.

### Data sources
- Public books and published excerpts.
- Peer-reviewed research and other publicly available, credible data sources.
- All visualized associations carry evidence type, source, and limitations. The project does not generate new clinical findings.

### Core purpose
Make the multi-dimensional links between diet, mechanisms, and health/disease outcomes easier for non-experts to explore and understand, while remaining transparent about evidence strength and limitations.

## Goal
Build a **professional-grade, product-level** open platform that makes these relationships visually explorable and understandable for ordinary people, while remaining scientifically transparent and evidence-based.

This is **not** a quick demo or academic prototype that will be abandoned. From day one we design for:

- Usability by non-experts
- Scientific integrity (evidence levels, limitations, sources clearly shown)
- Scalability of data and features
- Maintainability and clean architecture
- Clear disclaimers (not a medical device)

## Why This Exists
Most people choose food by habit and taste because the links between diet and health are hard to grasp. Existing tools tend to serve professional users with dense tables or specialist interfaces. ClearDiet occupies a different space: an interactive view where ordinary users can see pathways such as “animal protein / ultra-processed foods → chronic inflammation / oxidative stress → elevated risk of certain chronic conditions” and explore alternatives, without needing domain expertise.

## Quality Bar (Non-Negotiable)

- Professional UI/UX (mobile-friendly, accessible, clear narrative, concise prompts)
- Every visualized claim traces back to a source and evidence type
- Explicit labeling of correlations vs causation, ecological vs individual data
- Modular design so new public data sources can be added without rewriting the core
- Documentation and tests from the beginning

## Scope Boundaries
- Educational and research exploration tool for the general public
- No diagnostic claims, no treatment protocols, no supplement dosing
- No original deep research in nutrition or disease domains
- Personalized simulations, if added later, are clearly marked as educational models, not clinical advice

## Team & Ownership
Development is coordinated in this repository. Research literature notes and book excerpts live in the dedicated knowledge-base Project for the research assistant workflow. Product code, structured data, schemas, and open-data pipelines live here.
